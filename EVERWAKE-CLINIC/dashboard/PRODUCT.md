# Everwake Clinic: product

## Who uses it, and where

| Who | Where | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **The front desk** (receptionist, nurse) | At a desk with a laptop, during a busy evening session, in Arabic, with the phone ringing | To check people in, book and move them, answer WhatsApp when the assistant hands over, and see the waiting room |
| **The doctor** | Between patients, often on a phone, a minute at a time | Who is next and what not to miss (allergies, what the patient said, their last visit); a dental chart and plans; telling everyone they are running late with one action |
| **The owner or manager** | Setting the clinic up, then now and then | Prices the assistant quotes word for word, schedules, rules, reminders' wording, the team |
| **The patient** | Never signs in | One page from the reminder link: their appointment, and keep it / move it / cancel it |

## Mode

**Operate.** People complete tasks under time pressure. Scanability, consistency, keyboard and thumb reach, and the real usage scene outrank expression. Brand lives in precise details: the session ribbon, the ink colour, the copy.

## Principles

- **Today answers one question:** who is next, and is the day running on time?
- **The assistant does the talking; the dashboard shows where a person is needed.** Emergencies, the doctor's questions, no-replies and unconfirmed bookings come first.
- **Nothing reaches patients without a preview.** Running late, cancelling a session, a quote, publishing a price list.
- **Every change to what the assistant says can be undone.**
- **Arabic first.** Every screen is right-to-left, and the copy is Egyptian where staff speak it ("دخّله"، "ماجاش"). English is complete too.
- **Medical records are for clinical members only.** A receptionist sees a lock and a sentence, not an error.
