02 — Principles

Five commitments we make before anything is built

Baseline first

Week one measures the numbers the project will be judged on — confirmation time, recovery rate, conversion, order value, repeat purchase — against your own data, not an industry average. If we cannot measure it in week one, we will not claim it in month three.

Governed knowledge

Answers come from one controlled source of truth holding specifications, policies, pricing and commercial rules — including what the agent must never say. The never-say list is enforced at the knowledge layer, not left to the model's judgement.

Escalation by design

A clean handover to a person is built into the first workflow, with full context attached.

Ownership optional

Run it as a managed partnership, or take the whole system and pay nothing monthly. The build is identical either way — which means the decision is commercial, not technical, and you can make it late.

Measured monthly

We report against the week-one baseline. Targets come from your numbers, and the report says what moved and what did not.

One named owner. Every engagement needs one person on your side who can approve what the system is allowed to say. This is the single most reliable predictor of whether a project ships on time.

03 — Commercials

Two ways to own what we build

The build is identical under both. What differs is what happens after go-live.

Managed partnership

One-time implementation, then a monthly fee.

  • Hosting, monitoring and uptime checks
  • Continuous tuning and optimisation
  • Maintenance, bug fixes and upgrades
  • Knowledge base updated as products change
  • Priority support and monthly review
No monthly fee

Full ownership

Paid once. Handed over. No ongoing commitment.

  • Full transfer of workflows and configuration
  • Deployed to infrastructure you own
  • Complete technical documentation
  • Knowledge base and data exported in full
  • On-demand support, per request

Pricing is quoted per engagement, against the scope agreed in discovery. Under either option your data and knowledge base belong to you.
