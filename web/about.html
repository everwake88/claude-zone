About Everwake

An AI studio built around one idea.

The systems we build should belong to the business that runs them. We design them, build them, document them — and, if you prefer, hand them over completely.

Book a free demo What we build
01 — Who we are

The operating layer between your store, your customers and your team.

Everwake is an AI transformation and intelligent automation studio. We build systems that answer, confirm, follow up and escalate on their own, every hour of the day.

Our work centres on the conversations that cost a business most when they go unanswered — sales and orders on WhatsApp and the phone, often outside office hours, often in dialect. Around them we build the automation, custom AI, websites, CRMs and dashboards that turn those conversations into a running operation.

Whatever we build, the client owns the result. Under every arrangement, your data and knowledge base belong to you.

Ownership optional. Run it as a managed partnership, or take the whole system and pay nothing monthly. The build is identical either way, so the decision is commercial, not technical — and you can make it late.
