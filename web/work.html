01 — Case study

IQ Homes Qatar

A premium retailer of AI robots, smart home technology, beauty devices and accessories — selling in a category where customers must be educated before they can be sold to, mostly over WhatsApp, mostly outside office hours.

The build

A Qatari-Arabic AI sales and support agent running on WhatsApp and Instagram, connected to the client's Shopify catalogue, answering product, policy and pricing questions in dialect and handing over to the team when a person is needed.

What the system does

  • Identifies which product a customer means from an alias, a campaign link or the last thing they asked about — deterministically, before the model speaks
  • Loads product detail in tiers, so a simple question costs a fraction of a complex one and the catalogue still fits in context
  • Enforces a never-say list per product: claims the brand does not permit are blocked at the knowledge layer, not left to the model's judgement
  • Keeps conversation history and customer memory, so a returning buyer is not asked the same question twice
  • Escalates to the team with the full thread attached when the question is commercial, sensitive or unresolved

02 — Where it applies

The same platform, different vertical

The architecture does not change between industries. The knowledge base, the commercial rules and the dialect do.

Restaurants & QSR

Conversational ordering at branch scale: menu questions, option walking, cart pricing, address capture and a clean kitchen ticket.

Retail & e-commerce

Pre-sale education, specification questions, cross-sell at the right moment and abandoned-cart recovery that answers the objection.

Grocery & pharmacy

High-SKU catalogues where dialect-native matching and tiered retrieval decide whether the right item is ever found.

Home & field services

Qualification, scheduling and address validation before a vehicle is dispatched to the wrong street.

Clinics & booking

Appointment intake with strict policy boundaries and an escalation path that triggers on anything clinical.
