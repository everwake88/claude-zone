What we build

Five services. One knowledge base.

A client normally starts with one and the others attach to it, because they all draw on the same governed source of truth. Nothing here is a product you rent from us — it is a system we build, document and, if you want it, hand over.

01 — The services

What each one actually covers

01

Conversational AI agents

An agent that sells and supports on WhatsApp and Instagram, in the customer's own dialect.

Most useful when buying decisions need education before they need a price, and the conversation is already happening on WhatsApp.

  • Answers product questions with real specifications, images and video
  • Recommends alternatives, and says so when the fit is wrong
  • Carries conversation history and customer memory between chats
  • Hands over to a person the moment a human is genuinely needed

02

AI commerce platform

The full post-checkout and pre-checkout lifecycle, automated end to end.

Most useful when order volume has outgrown the team confirming it by hand, and the cost of a wrong address is a redelivery.

  • Order confirmation within minutes of checkout, written back to the store
  • Address and contact validation before dispatch, exceptions flagged with context
  • Delivery follow-up, review requests, cross-sell at the right moment
  • Abandoned-cart recovery that answers the objection instead of nagging

03

Knowledge engineering

The part most projects skip. Scattered product and policy content becomes a governed, measurable knowledge base.

This is the difference between an agent that sounds right and an agent that is right. It is where the governance lives.

  • Tiered retrieval so the right detail loads at the right moment, at controlled cost
  • Never-say rules, pricing rules and policy boundaries enforced at the source
  • Answer coverage tested against a real question bank and reported as a number

04

Workflow automation & integration

The plumbing that makes the rest real: store, messaging, database, operations and reporting, wired together.

Most useful when the data already exists but lives in four places that do not talk to each other.

  • Shopify and commerce APIs, WhatsApp Business Platform, Meta Business Suite
  • Operations alerts, sheets and dashboards for the team that runs the day
  • Self-hosted orchestration on infrastructure you can own

05

AI marketing studio

Scoped separately, once the core platform is live.

We only take this on after the commerce side is running, because the brand voice it generates from should be the one the agent already speaks.

  • Image, video and copy generation in the brand's voice
  • Scheduled publishing to Meta channels
