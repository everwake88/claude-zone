01 — Who we are

Most AI projects stall in the same place.

A demo impresses, then nobody can run it, nobody knows what it will say to a real customer, and nobody owns it.

We work the other way around. We baseline the numbers before we build. We govern what the system is allowed to say. We design the handover to a human into the first workflow. And we leave the client able to run the system without us.

What we are not. We are not a chatbot vendor, and we do not resell someone else's platform. We build the system, document it, and — if you prefer — transfer it outright with no monthly subscription. Your data and knowledge base belong to you under every arrangement.

03 — How we work differently

Five commitments we make before anything is built.

Baseline first

Week one measures the numbers the project will be judged on — confirmation time, recovery rate, conversion, order value, repeat purchase — against your own data, not an industry average.

Governed knowledge

Answers come from one controlled source of truth holding specifications, policies, pricing and commercial rules — including what the agent must never say.

Escalation by design

A clean handover to a person is built into the first workflow, with full context attached. Not bolted on after launch.

Ownership optional

Run it as a managed partnership, or take the whole system and pay nothing monthly. The build is identical either way.

Measured monthly

We report against the week-one baseline. Targets come from your numbers, and the report says what moved and what did not.

Delivery is remote-first from Egypt, with clients in Qatar and the wider Gulf. Systems are built to work in Arabic — including Gulf dialect — and English, on WhatsApp, Instagram and the web.
