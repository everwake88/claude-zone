Working with us

What does Everwake actually do?

We design, build and hand over AI systems that run the repetitive parts of a business: voice and chat agents that sell to and support your customers, automation around orders and follow-up, custom AI built on your own data, and the websites, CRMs and dashboards that connect it all.

How long does a project take?

A typical engagement runs four weeks from kick-off to production: discovery in week one, platform and knowledge base in week two, workflows and testing in week three, and go-live with training in week four. Larger and custom builds are scoped individually.

How much does it cost?

Every project is priced against the scope agreed in discovery, so we don't publish a price list. Book a meeting, see a free demo, and you will receive a written proposal with a fixed price — with no retainer and no obligation.

What is included in the free demo?

A short session where we show the agent answering real questions about your own products and policies. You see how it sounds, how it sells and how it hands over to your team before you commit to anything.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to the store or system the automation reads from, a verified business messaging account or phone line, the product, pricing and policy content the knowledge base is built from — in whatever shape it exists today — and one person on your side who can approve what the system is allowed to say.

The agents

Which languages and dialects do your agents speak?

Arabic, including Gulf and Egyptian dialects, and English. Spelling variants, mixed Arabic and English, and product names written either way are all understood as the same thing.

Which channels do you work on?

WhatsApp, Instagram, your website and the phone. One knowledge base sits behind all of them, so a customer gets the same answer wherever they ask.

Will the AI say something wrong to my customers?

Answers come from one governed knowledge base that includes what the agent must never say. Prices and totals are calculated in code, not by the model, and every reply is checked before it is sent. When a question falls outside what it knows, it hands over to a person.

What happens when a customer needs a real person?

The conversation moves to your team with its full history attached, so nothing is retyped and nothing is lost. Escalation is built into the first workflow, not added after launch.

Does this replace my team?

No. It removes the repetitive work that fills their day — answering the same questions, taking orders, confirming addresses — so their attention goes to the customers and decisions that genuinely need a person.

Ownership and data

Can I own the system outright?

Yes. You can run it as a managed partnership with a monthly fee, or take full ownership: the workflows, configuration and knowledge base are transferred to infrastructure you own, with complete documentation and no monthly subscription. The build is identical either way.

Who owns our data?

You do, under every arrangement. Systems are encrypted, access-controlled and fully logged, and your data and knowledge base can be exported in full at any time.

Do you work outside Qatar and Egypt?

Yes. Delivery is remote-first, with clients in Qatar, the wider Gulf and Egypt, and we take on projects worldwide.
