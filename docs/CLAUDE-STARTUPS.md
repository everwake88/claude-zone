# Claude for Startups — every requirement and term

Anthropic's startup programme. It gives qualifying young companies Claude API
credits, discounted Claude seats, partner discounts and direct access to
Anthropic's Applied AI team.

This page collects **everything you have to qualify for, everything you have to
do, and everything you agree to** — in plain English, with a link to the
official wording for each point.

**Checked: 11 October 2026.** The programme changed twice in the last week.
Re-read the official pages before you apply.

---

## ⚠️ Read this first — the two best offers are paused

On **6 October 2026** Anthropic expanded the programme: a free year of Claude
Team and $1,000 in API credits.

Within 48 hours it received **21× more applications than in the previous five
months combined**, and by **9 October** the programme page said:

> "We're currently over capacity on the Claude Team and $1,000 API credit
> offers… If you've already claimed the offer, nothing changes… we'll re-review
> all applications which may result in a change in the status of some
> applications."

What that means for you:

| | |
|---|---|
| Already claimed the offer | You keep it |
| Approved but not yet claimed | Being re-reviewed — you may lose it |
| Applying now | The credits and Team seats are **not available**; the rest of the programme is |
| Still open to everyone | Startup Stack partner offers, Applied AI office hours, events, higher rate limits (for members who hold credits) |

Anthropic has published **no date** for when the paused offers return. Applying
now is still worth it — membership itself carries the Stack and office hours —
but do not plan a budget around the $1,000.

## Part 1 — The requirements

### A. Your company

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Company age / funding | **Founded within the last 5 years, or raised funding within the last 2 years.** Announced on 6 Oct 2026 and reported everywhere, but **no longer printed on the programme page** — treat it as the working rule, not a guarantee |
| Funding stage | **No VC needed.** "The program is open to bootstrapped, pre-seed, and venture-backed startups" |
| Building with Claude | Not a hard gate, but it is one of the three things they judge you on |
| Previously received Anthropic startup credits | Older terms excluded repeat recipients. Not stated in the current text — assume one bite |

Everwake is well inside the five-year window, and being bootstrapped is
explicitly fine. Nothing in the company test rules you out.

### B. Your country — Everwake is fine

Two separate lists have to be satisfied, and **Egypt passes both**:

1. **Anthropic's supported countries.** Egypt is on it. The rule covers both
   where you are physically located *and* where the company is incorporated or
   headquartered.
2. **The programme's exclusion list.** You are ineligible if you operate in,
   are established in, or plan to offer Anthropic's services in: **Belarus,
   China, Cuba, Iran, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia, Sudan, Syria, Crimea, or
   the so-called Donetsk or Luhansk People's Republics.** Egypt is not listed.

You are also ineligible if US or other applicable export-control law prohibits
you from receiving funds or services.

### C. Who can never join

Anthropic employees, officers, directors, agents and representatives, their
immediate families and household members, and anyone involved in running the
programme.

### D. What you need in hand before you apply

- [ ] A **Claude Console account** — `console.anthropic.com`, the developer
      console. **Not** the Claude chat app. This is the account the benefits
      attach to, so create it in the company's name, not a personal one.
- [ ] A **company email address**. Widely reported requirement: it must match
      your website's domain — so `hello@everwake.tech`, not a Gmail address.
      Not printed on the current page, but assume it.
- [ ] Your **website**, live, at that domain.
- [ ] Founding date, funding status, headcount.
- [ ] A short description of **what you are building with Claude**.
- [ ] One named **contact person** — all correspondence goes to them and only
      them.

> The site is on `everwake.tech`. If you do not yet have an address **at**
> that domain — `hello@everwake.tech` rather than a Gmail one — set that up
> before applying: [GO-LIVE.md](GO-LIVE.md) covers it in the email section.

### E. How to apply, and what they judge

1. Sign in to the Claude Console.
2. Fill in the application on
   [claude.com/programs/startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups).
   It takes a couple of minutes.
3. Anthropic aims to **decide within one week** and emails the result. Simple
   cases are reportedly automatic; the rest go to manual review.

Stated evaluation factors: **business traction, investment and funding, and
Claude integration and usage.**

Hard truths from the official terms:

- The application must be **fully complete and compliant** — incomplete ones
  may be disqualified.
- **Eligibility is at Anthropic's sole discretion**, and "all interpretations
  of these Terms and decisions made by Anthropic relating to the Program are
  final and binding in all respects."
- Meeting every criterion **does not entitle you to anything**.
- Existing members do not reapply — they redeem offers in the Console.

## Part 3 — The terms you are agreeing to

Four documents stack on top of each other. In a conflict about the extra
benefits, the Addendum wins.

| Document | Dated | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| [Startup Program Official Terms](https://anthropic.com/startup-program-official-terms) | 22 May 2025 | Eligibility, selection, obligations, liability |
| [Claude Startups Additional Benefits Addendum](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/startup-program-addendum) | 6 Oct 2026 | Stack, office hours, promotional credits |
| [Credit Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/credit-terms) | — | How all credits behave |
| Usage Policy + Commercial Terms | — | Normal rules for using the API at all |

### Credits

- **Expiry.** The $1,000 was reported to expire **6 months** after it is
  granted. The general rule: promotional credits expire on the date given when
  issued, or **1 calendar year** after issue if no date is given. Purchased
  usage credits expire 1 year from issue.
- **First-party only.** Credits work on the Claude API through the Claude
  Console. They **cannot** be used on AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI or
  any other third-party platform.
- **Non-refundable.** Always.
- **No cash value.** "Credits are not legal tender or currency of any kind."
- **Non-transferable.** You may not transfer or sell them; only the holder of
  the associated account may use them.
- **Close the account and they vanish** — automatically, unrecoverably.
- When credits run out, **standard API pricing applies automatically**. No
  warning step, no migration. Watch this.
- Credits may be **taxed differently from cash**. Anthropic makes no
  representation about tax treatment, issues no tax forms unless legally
  required, and **may forfeit your credits if you do not supply required tax
  documentation**. Ask your accountant.

### Claude Team offer

Reported conditions, not all of them in the published terms: one year, up to
five Premium seats, **only for organisations that have never had Claude Team**,
claimed separately, and some sources report a two-seat minimum and a card on
file. Verify in the Console at redemption time.

### Startup Stack — read this twice

- Anthropic **does not create, fund, administer or guarantee** these offers and
  is not a party to any deal between you and a provider. Listing is not
  endorsement.
- **Each provider's own terms, pricing and privacy policy govern.** You
  contract with them, and data you hand over is theirs to handle.
- Unless a provider says otherwise, each benefit is **redeemable once per
  member, non-transferable, has no cash value**, and cannot be sold, combined
  with other offers, or used where prohibited.
- **You may not share codes or links outside your company**, or scrape them.
  Breach this and Anthropic can revoke your Stack access and tell the provider.
- Providers can change or withdraw offers at any time, and Anthropic can remove
  benefits or the whole page without notice.

### Applied AI office hours — the clause to watch

- **Consultative only.** You remain solely responsible for your products,
  systems and decisions. Anthropic commits to **no specific amount of support**
  and may decline, modify or discontinue at any time.
- You may use any advice you receive for any purpose, at your own risk.
- Anthropic **keeps ownership** of its methods and frameworks; you get no
  licence beyond what is embedded in the advice.
- **Residuals.** Anthropic may use general know-how its people retain in
  unaided memory from your confidential information, may build competing
  technology, and may assign the same people to other customers.

> For a studio like Everwake this is the one real risk. **Do not take a
> client's confidential material into an office-hours session.** Describe your
> own architecture, abstract the client out. You almost certainly have NDAs that
> this clause would cut across.

### What you take on as a member

- If selected you may be required to sign a **compliance affidavit and a
  publicity and liability release within 14 calendar days** of being asked —
  miss it and you forfeit the benefits.
- You grant Anthropic permission to **use your name and logo** publicly to
  identify you as an awardee and customer (except where law forbids).
- You must **consider in good faith** requests for an executive quote and for
  public co-marketing.
- You must **keep your contact details current**. Anthropic is not responsible
  if a stale address means you miss a notice.
- You must remain **in good standing and compliant with all Anthropic terms** —
  including the Usage Policy — or benefits end.
- Benefits attach to the **company named in the application**, not to you.

### What Anthropic reserves

- Cancel, terminate, modify or suspend the programme **at its sole
  discretion** — which is exactly what happened on 9 October.
- Correct errors, including benefits awarded by mistake.
- **You may not bring claims** arising from cancellation, suspension or
  amendment.
- Everything is **"as is"** and "as available", with no warranties of any kind
  and no guarantee of uninterrupted service or fitness for your purpose.
- **Total liability is capped at the lesser of the actual value of the benefits
  awarded or $1,000.**
- **California law**, disputes exclusively in **San Francisco**, and you waive
  the right to seek injunctive relief as far as law permits.
- Anthropic may **modify the Addendum at any time without notice**.

## Part 4 — What this actually means for Everwake

1. **Apply anyway.** Membership costs nothing, and the Stack and office hours
   are live even while the headline offers are paused. Worst case: a no.
2. **Apply from a company Console account** on `everwake.tech`, with the
   company as applicant. A personal account cannot be unwound later.
3. **Budget as if the $1,000 never arrives.** It is paused, and if it does
   arrive it may die in six months. Credits are a discount on a bill you can
   already afford, never the basis of a client quote.
4. **Never price client work against credits.** They expire, they are
   non-transferable, and they are revocable at Anthropic's discretion. When
   they run out, full API pricing starts silently.
5. **Keep client confidentiality out of office hours** — see the residuals
   clause above.
6. **Expect to appear in Anthropic's marketing.** For a young studio that is
   upside, but know you have agreed to it.
7. **Diarise the credit expiry** the day it lands.
8. **Re-check the page before applying.** This programme changed twice in four
   days.
