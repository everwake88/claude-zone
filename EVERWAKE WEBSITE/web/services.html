What we build

Eight services. One knowledge base.

Most clients start with one service and add the others, because they all draw on the same governed source of truth. Nothing here is a product you rent from us — it is a system we build, document and, if you want it, hand over.

Book a free demo See the bundles

01

Voice agents

AI agents that answer and place phone calls in Arabic — including Gulf and Egyptian dialects — and English. They book, confirm, qualify and follow up, and hand the call to a person the moment one is needed.

Best for: Clinics, restaurants, real estate and service businesses whose customers still pick up the phone.

Discuss voice agents
  • Inbound calls answered around the clock — questions, bookings and order status
  • Outbound calls for confirmations, reminders, follow-up and lead qualification
  • Natural turn-taking that copes with interruptions and changes of mind
  • Every call recorded, transcribed, summarised and logged to your CRM
  • A warm transfer to your team, with a summary of the call so far

02

Chat agents

A sales and support agent on WhatsApp, Instagram and your website that recommends like your best salesperson, prices like your point of sale, and never forgets what a customer already told it.

Best for: Retail, restaurants and any business already taking orders on WhatsApp.

Discuss chat agents See how the chat agent works →
  • Answers product questions with real specifications, images and video
  • Recommends across a price range and walks every required option
  • Prices every order in code from your catalogue — it cannot invent a price
  • Remembers each customer between conversations
  • Hands over to your team with the full conversation attached

03

AI automation solutions

The repetitive operations around every sale, automated end to end and written back to the systems you already use.

Best for: Businesses whose order volume has outgrown the team confirming it by hand.

Discuss automation
  • Order confirmation within minutes of checkout, written back to your store
  • Address and contact validation before dispatch, with exceptions flagged
  • Delivery follow-up, review requests and cross-sell at the right moment
  • Abandoned-cart recovery that answers the objection instead of nagging
  • Alerts, sheets and reports for the team that runs the day

04

Custom AI solutions

When the problem is specific to your business, we build the system around your data, your rules and your workflows — and document it so your team can run it.

Best for: Teams with a clear process, scattered knowledge, and nothing off the shelf that fits.

Discuss a custom build
  • Governed knowledge bases, with never-say and pricing rules enforced at the source
  • Internal assistants that answer from your own documents, policies and data
  • Tiered retrieval and multi-model routing to keep running costs under control
  • Answer quality measured against a real question bank and reported as a number
  • Deployed on infrastructure you can own outright

05

Websites & CRMs

Fast, bilingual websites built to turn visitors into conversations — and a CRM set up around how you actually sell, so no lead falls through a gap.

Best for: Businesses whose leads live in WhatsApp chats, spreadsheets and people's memories.

Discuss a website or CRM
  • Websites in Arabic and English, mobile first and built to load fast
  • CRM setup or migration, with pipelines that match your sales process
  • Every form, chat and call lands in the CRM automatically
  • Your agent, store and CRM sharing one view of each customer
  • Dashboards that show where every lead came from and where it stands

06

Smart solutions

Three kinds of intelligence for the rest of the business: dashboards that explain your numbers, documents that read themselves, and devices that act on their own.

Best for: Operations drowning in reports, paperwork, or devices that should talk to each other.

Discuss smart solutions
  • Live dashboards and AI reporting on your sales and operations data
  • Forecasts and alerts that fire when a number moves, not when someone notices
  • Invoices, contracts, IDs and forms read, checked and filed automatically
  • Smart home and IoT integrations for connected devices, sensors and buildings
  • All of it feeding the same knowledge base your agents answer from

Marketing & advertising

Content and campaigns produced in your brand's voice, published on schedule, and measured by the conversations and orders they create — not by likes.

Best for: Brands that want marketing and sales to finally share the same numbers.

  • Images, video and copy generated in your brand's voice
  • Campaigns on Meta channels, scheduled and published automatically

  • Reporting that ties spend to conversations and orders, not just clicks
  • Best taken on once your agent or automation is already live
